# Code of Conduct

Sprintline welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Treat others with respect, keep feedback focused on the work, and give people room to ask questions and disagree.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing someone’s private information without consent are unacceptable. This policy applies to the project’s issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.

To report a concern, contact [@boredpolymath](https://github.com/boredpolymath). If you need a private channel, you can open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/boredpolymath/Sprintline/issues/new) asking the maintainer to arrange one. Keep names, evidence, and incident details out of the public issue.

Maintainers will review reports and share details only as needed to respond. Depending on the conduct, maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, or restrict participation.
